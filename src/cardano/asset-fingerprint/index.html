🖐️ Asset Fingerprint

Compute a native asset's fingerprint (CIP-14), the short asset1… ID that explorers and wallets show.

How it works

A native asset is identified by its policy ID (the hash of its minting policy) and its name (up to 32 bytes). CIP-14 hashes the two together with Blake2b-160 and encodes the result in bech32 with the asset prefix. Different assets can never share a fingerprint, but the fingerprint alone doesn't tell you the policy or name.
