🌱 Mnemonic Accounts

Derive keys and addresses from a recovery phrase the way Shelley wallets do (CIP-1852: Yoroi, Eternl, Lace, Daedalus).

Runs locally in your browser. Don't paste keys that hold real funds.

How it works

The phrase's entropy goes through PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 (4096 rounds, the passphrase as password) to make the root key. Keys are then derived with BIP32-Ed25519 along m/1852'/1815'/account'/role/index: role 0 is receiving addresses, 1 is change, 2 is the stake key. Each base address combines a payment key hash with the account's stake key hash.

Legacy Byron (Daedalus) and Ledger/Trezor hardware-wallet roots use other schemes, so their addresses will differ.
